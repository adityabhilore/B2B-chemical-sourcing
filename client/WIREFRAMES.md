# Phase 2 wireframes

All copy and business data shown below are placeholders until replaced with approved company content. Do not publish unverified statistics, certifications, brands, delivery promises, or safety claims.

## Home

1. Sticky public header with brand, Products/Categories/Blog/Contact links, catalogue search, account actions, and an accessible mobile drawer.
2. Hero on a navy surface: short positioning headline, supporting placeholder copy, name/CAS/category search, and catalogue/enquiry actions.
3. Trust strip reserved for verified company statistics or certifications. Keep it omitted or neutral until real facts and approved assets are supplied.
4. Featured products in a responsive four-column card grid, with image, category, product name, CAS number when supplied, and price/quote action.
5. Dynamic categories grid sourced from the catalogue API; show an empty state when no categories are available.
6. Brands strip populated only from approved brand records and logos; omit when empty.
7. Ordering steps in four concise cards, with process wording confirmed by the business.
8. Latest published blog cards, followed by a contact/enquiry CTA.
9. Rich footer with company summary, verified contact details, catalogue/account links, and legal links.

## Products listing

1. Public header and breadcrumb.
2. Page title, concise placeholder intro, result count, and search by product name/CAS number.
3. Desktop filter sidebar and mobile collapsible filters for category, grade, availability, and other API-supported facets.
4. Sort control and responsive product card grid; include loading skeletons, no-results state, and error/retry state.
5. Pagination with current page and result range.

## Product details

1. Breadcrumb and back-to-catalogue link.
2. Two-column product summary: image gallery, product name, CAS/grade/category metadata, and price or request-a-quote action.
3. Variant/pack-size selector and quantity entry when product data supports them.
4. Tabs for description, specifications, documents, and handling information; show only supplied and approved data.
5. Related products and enquiry CTA.

## Blog

1. Public header and breadcrumb.
2. Blog title, placeholder introduction, and search/category filters.
3. Featured article followed by published article cards with date, author, topic, and excerpt.
4. Pagination and clear empty/loading/error states.
5. Article detail uses a readable text column, metadata, cover image, article content, and related posts.

## Contact

1. Public header, breadcrumb, and concise enquiry invitation.
2. Contact form with name, company, work email, phone (optional), enquiry type, and message; labels, hints, inline validation, and submission feedback.
3. Contact details, office address, and hours only after supplied and verified.
4. Map/embed only when a real address is provided; accessible text alternative.

## Admin Dashboard

1. Collapsible admin sidebar, top bar with user menu, and breadcrumbs.
2. KPI cards populated from the API; use skeletons and never substitute invented figures.
3. Order activity chart only when backed by real data, with a text summary.
4. Recent orders table with search, sortable columns, status pills, row actions, pagination, and loading/empty/error states.
5. Catalogue and content shortcuts.

## Admin Add Product

1. Admin shell, breadcrumb, page title, and save draft/publish actions.
2. Main form sections: product identity (name, slug, CAS, category, brand), description, grade/specifications, and variants/pricing/packaging.
3. Media and document uploads with file type/size guidance, preview, remove/reorder controls, and accessible labels.
4. Publication/status and inventory settings in a side panel on wide screens and inline section on mobile.
5. Sticky or repeated save actions, validation summary, and confirmation feedback; validation errors remain associated with each field.
